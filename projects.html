01

National Innovation Portal (RICC)

Federal Ministry of Education, Nigeria · Frontend & UI/UX Lead · ricc.education.gov.ng

Result A live national platform, prepared for a national rollout targeting 2,000+ institutional responses, that collects data even where there is no internet.

The problem. Nigeria had no reliable picture of how research moves from universities, institutes and innovators into the market. Data came in through Google Forms, often from places with weak or no internet, and the same innovation was reported again and again. Nobody could see where promising work was getting stuck.

What I did

  • Researched why universities, industry, investors and regulators weren't connecting, and turned the findings into user journeys and questionnaires for each stakeholder group.
  • Led frontend development of an offline-first web app: field agents complete long questionnaires with no connection, answers are kept on the device and sync automatically once they are back online.
  • Built smart questionnaires that hide questions that don't apply, with word limits, ranked choices, state-to-local-government location logic, consent tracking and reference numbers.
  • Built the analytics dashboards (readiness scorecards, funding maps, participation by region and a "valley of death" chart of where innovations drop off), an evidence-verification panel for the secretariat, a chatbot that answers with live charts, and terms, consent and privacy controls.
  • Ran end-to-end testing on staging before every production release; historical data was cleaned so 62 submissions became 298 distinct innovations with 105 duplicates removed.
  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • offline web app (service workers)
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL

NGAO: WhatsApp Data Collection

Last-mile reporting, Kenya · Full-Stack Developer

Result Rolled out and used by more than 3,000 people.

The problem. Field reporting such as tree planting and seedling sales ran on paper and informal messages: slow, hard to verify and easy to fake.

What I did

  • Built the engines and orchestrators that run each conversation: button-led flows in English and Kiswahili that take text, photos, voice notes and location.
  • Built answer correction that checks a dictionary first, falls back to AI, asks "Did you mean…?" and learns from every confirmation, so messy answers become clean data.
  • Compared each photo's location data with the shared location and flagged mismatches, and kept every submission as a permanent record.
  • Connected the backend to the admin dashboards and checked dashboard figures against the raw records and CSV export; a schema builder lets administrators launch new pipelines without new code.
  • NestJS
  • PostgreSQL
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • WhatsApp Business API
  • Twilio (voice and images)
  • LLMs

PropFlow: Property Management Platform

Landlords and managing agents, East Africa · Lead Engineer & Product Lead

Result In production with users in Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda; partners met in both countries and five CEOs and their CTOs trained in Rwanda.

The problem. Landlords run rent, tenants and repairs across spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and paper files, so money and documents slip through the cracks.

What I did

  • Designed and built, with a partner, one system for the whole tenancy: rent roll and billing, arrears, late fees, deposits and reconciliation, leases generated and signed online, repairs with contractor approvals, online payments and a full audit log.
  • Tenants report issues on WhatsApp with photos; emergencies trigger an automatic phone call.
  • AI proposes the next step from tenant messages, and every suggestion is checked against business rules before anything happens.
  • Shipped production-grade: containerised deployment, background scheduler, nightly backups, error monitoring, automated tests and a written recovery plan. I maintain it.
  • Travelled to Uganda and Rwanda to meet partner companies, including the leadership of Keystone Technologies and Unispace, and trained five CEOs, property owners and their CTOs in Rwanda.
