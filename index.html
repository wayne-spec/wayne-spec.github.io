Nairobi City County · Pneuma Labs · 2026

Ask the county's revenue data a question on WhatsApp

Problem
Revenue reports arrived as Excel workbooks in several different layouts. Answering a simple question, such as what one revenue stream brought in last quarter, meant someone digging through spreadsheets by hand.
What I did
Wrote and tested parsers for seven sheet layouts that load every upload into Postgres. Built an AI agent that answers plain-language questions on the web and on WhatsApp. It can only run validated, read-only queries through a restricted database role, and only approved numbers can reach it. I ran several accuracy audits against the source sheets and fixed double-counting and date-range errors.
Result
Staff get answers computed from the county's own figures in a chat message, without opening a spreadsheet.

Market intelligence · Pneuma Labs · 2026

An AI research analyst for the Nairobi Securities Exchange

Problem
Analysts covering the NSE spend most of their time gathering prices, macro data and news before they can start thinking about a stock.
What I did
Built a multi-agent research system, adapted to TypeScript from the open-source TradingAgents framework. Analyst agents feed a bull-and-bear debate, a trader and a risk check, and every stock is scored against an inflation-adjusted return hurdle. Each prediction is logged and resolved later, so the system's accuracy can be measured rather than assumed. Model calls fall back across four LLM providers so the daily run still completes when one goes down.
Result
Analysts in Kenya use it for research and decision support. It speeds up their research; the investment decision stays with them.
